# TNF-alpha epitope candidates (P3)

Generated by `scripts/tnfa_epitope_sasa.py` (Shrake-Rupley, probe 1.4 A, 400 points) on `data/1TNF_ABC_protein.pdb`. All values COMPUTED.

Rule: a residue is a groove/rim candidate if trimerization buries more than 10 A^2 of it and it keeps at least 20 A^2 exposed in the trimer. Counts per chain: A 27, B 24, C 21 (`data/epitope_candidates.json`). A first run gave 0/0/21 because of a bug (single-chain results overwritten); fixed before use.

## Conserved human/mouse exposed interface positions found on at least two chains
S9, K11, A33, N34, L36, L75, N92, A109, K112, P113, Y115, E116, Y119, Q125, S147, L157 (1TNF numbering, UniProt = number + 76).

## Positions that differ in mouse (avoid as hotspots if cross-reactivity matters)

## Proposed hotspot sets for the design runs
- E1 (primary): Y115, Y119, K112, E116, N92, L75, Q125 plus S147 from the neighbouring chain. Aromatic cluster, conserved.
- E2 (alternative): A33, N34, L36, S9, K11, L157. More polar and flatter.

Not verified: that E1/E2 overlap the receptor-binding site. TNFR1/adalimumab/infliximab complexes are not in this checkout. The page only says the site lies between adjacent protomers. Check against a complex before relying on E1/E2.

## pH-switch note (hypothesis, not a rule from the page)
Native histidines: H15 (buried), H73 (exposed, but not conserved in mouse). The switch has to come from the binder side, for example His placed against E116 or at a salt-bridge/H-bond that breaks near pH 6. How to enforce this in design is for P4.
